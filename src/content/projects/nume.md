## The idea

A wellness platform that brings sleep, stress, activity, and personal check-ins into one member-facing experience.

## How it works

The frontend uses React and TypeScript. A FastAPI backend coordinates specialist agents through Google ADK, with PostgreSQL for persistence and Clerk for authentication.

## A closer look

The orchestration separates coordination, specialist routing, and validation so recommendations can be traced through the system. Docker and Cloudflare support the deployment workflow.
